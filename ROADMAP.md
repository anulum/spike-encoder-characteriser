# Scope and implemented state

## Present

Repository validation tooling and architecture documentation are present. Foundation qualification remains in progress. Implemented domain capabilities and scientific claims are empty.

## Intended characterisation surface

The planned tool measures seven actual imported software encoder families under complete continuous-source, decoder, statistic and comparison protocols. It retains every grid point and failed or inconclusive original-record outcome. Comparable clouds and fronts require the same admitted targets, representation, clocks and budgets. Fabric timing, provider integration, licensed datasets and physical measurements require their own actual evidence before support is claimed.
